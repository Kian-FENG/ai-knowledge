L12: # October 20, 2026
L13: ## Introducing locked chats
L14: Locked chats let you keep selected conversations in your account while hiding them from regular chat history, pinned chats, and search. Open them with your 4-digit account PIN or supported device authentication. Your retention and model-improvement settings stay unchanged.
L15: Locked chats are rolling out gradually to Free, Go, Plus, and Pro users on the web, in the iOS and Android apps, and in the cite2†new ChatGPT desktop app for macOS and Windows. To lock a chat, open a chat’s more options menu (•••) and select Lock chat.

L17: # October 16, 2026
L18: ## Create and collaborate on slides in ChatGPT
L19:
L20: ChatGPT Slides lets you create presentations and keep working on them with your team in Space. Start from a conversation, files, or an outline, or share an example deck to guide the style and layout.
L21:
L22:   * Create and refine. Edit text, images, shapes, and charts directly, or ask ChatGPT to make changes. Generate custom images or add your own.
L23:
L24:   * Work together. Invite teammates to edit the same deck in real time and leave shared comments.
L25:   * Present or export. Present directly from ChatGPT. On the web, export to PowerPoint, PDF, or Google Slides. In the desktop app, open your deck in PowerPoint or Keynote when those apps are installed.
L26:
L27: Slides is rolling out to eligible Pro, Business, and Enterprise users with Space access on the web and in the desktop app. To get started, ask ChatGPT in Work to create a presentation in Space.
