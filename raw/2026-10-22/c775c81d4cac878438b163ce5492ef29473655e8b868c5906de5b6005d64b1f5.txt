Oct 20 (Reuters) - ByteDance, Alibaba and Tencent have ordered more than 3 million of Nvidia's new RTX PRO 5500 graphics cards with a collective sticker price of over $35 billion, two people familiar with the matter said.
Nvidia has told customers and partners it cannot guarantee that it can fill the orders because demand far exceeds planned production, the two sources and a third person familiar with the matter said.
One source said Nvidia told partners they might receive only 5% to 10% of the quantities requested. Another said the company had indicated it could produce no more than 3 million RTX PRO 5500 cards globally in 2027. Total orders from customers in China have exceeded 6 million, two sources said.
Reuters attributed primary inference use and about $11,400 per card to anonymous sources. ByteDance, Alibaba and Tencent did not respond; Nvidia commented on broader restrictions and competitor growth without independently confirming these exact order totals. Reuters reports December samples and Q1 2027 commercial deliveries as expectations from sources; orders are not shipment or booked revenue.
