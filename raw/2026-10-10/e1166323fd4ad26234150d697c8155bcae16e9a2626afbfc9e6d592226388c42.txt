Sophos Fusion combines telemetry and third-party integrations with MDR. Investigation agents collect context, indicators and intelligence; a planning model plans, executes and reviews, producing a summary and recommended actions for analyst review. Customer operating modes Notify, Collaborate and Authorise determine who takes action; potentially destructive actions retain appropriate human oversight. Sophos reports average resolution time for agent-handled cases falling from 38 minutes to 89 seconds (96% reduction), and 52% of MDR cases resolved end-to-end by AI within calibrated boundaries.
