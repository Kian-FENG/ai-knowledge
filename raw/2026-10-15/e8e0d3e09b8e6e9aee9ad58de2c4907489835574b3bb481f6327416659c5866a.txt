L15: The center: the average eval score. Most teams hill‑climb this number, and it can rise while real failures stay put.

L25: ### Third moment

L27: The tail: the rare, hard cases an average smooths over. We build evals that target them. This is where we climb.

L34:

L37:   2. ### Diagnose
L38: 
L39: We look through those trajectories for failure modes and show them in a dashboard, with how often each one happens and real examples.

L45:   4. ### Improve

L48:   5. ### Ship
L49: 
L50: You get a new endpoint, plus the eval results that show what changed.
