L165: # Release Notes
L166: 
L167: > Dynamo v1.6.0-glm-5.3-flash-dev.1 is an experimental snapshot build with early GLM-5.3-Flash support on the Dynamo SGLang backend. It is not recommended for production and is not a QA-gated release. APIs, behavior, and defaults may change before stable support. Use it for evaluation, testing, and early feedback only.
L168: ## Summary
L169: Dynamo v1.6.0-glm-5.3-flash-dev.1 serves `RadixArk/GLM-5.3-Flash-NVFP4` on Dynamo SGLang. The container is built from `lmsysorg/sglang:v0.5.21-cu130-runtime` (CUDA 13) and applies GLM-5.3-Flash backports for prefill/decode accounting, linear-attention cache handling, SM100 PTX KDA, and Mamba slot cleanup, plus a scoped cuDNN workaround in the vision encoder.
L170: Transformers 5.19.0 and tokenizers 0.23.2 are pinned so image and video requests load `Glm5NextProcessor` instead of falling back to text-only tokenization (cite75†#15751 ).
L171: Kubernetes recipes cover a 4-GPU aggregated profile on B200 or GB200 and a 12-GPU GB200 disaggregated profile (two TP4/EP4 prefill workers and one TP4 decode worker). Aggregated serving uses round-robin routing and accepts text, images, and video. Disaggregated serving uses KV-aware routing with worker KV events and Mooncake transfer over NVLink, and accepts text and images. All three profiles use NVFP4 weights, FP8 KV cache, EAGLE speculative decoding with ReplaySSM, and HiCache.
L172: A shared model-cache download pins revision `f46cf340d35a22d0d83d0c1dac8957cf2b1bcd35`, and an AIPerf text-trace reference ships with the recipes (cite76†#15752 , cite77†#16387 ).
L173: Release Branch: `release/1.6.0-glm-5.3-flash-dev.1`, tip `6beb56900f9c83c3d7720432006ffc42d0c328c2` (2026-10-14)
L174: SGLang: `lmsysorg/sglang:v0.5.21-cu130-runtime` (CUDA 13.0); disaggregated KV transfer uses Mooncake over NVLink

L190: ## About GLM-5.3-Flash
L191: 
L192: GLM-5.3-Flash is a Z.AI mixture-of-experts model (320B total parameters, 18B active) with hybrid sparse and linear attention and Manifold-Constrained Hyper-Connections. This preview serves the NVFP4 checkpoint `RadixArk/GLM-5.3-Flash-NVFP4` through Dynamo's SGLang backend, aggregated on B200 or GB200 or disaggregated on GB200.
L193: 
L194: ## Full Changelog
L195: ### SGLang Backend
L196:   * GLM-5.3-Flash runtime: Builds the CUDA SGLang image on `lmsysorg/sglang:v0.5.21-cu130-runtime` and applies upstream backports for prefill/decode latency and retraction accounting, linear-attention hooks and cache accounting, PTX KDA safety and SM100 support, and Mamba slot cleanup before decode preallocation. A local patch disables cuDNN only around the GLM vision downsample convolution.
L197: Transformers 5.19.0 and tokenizers 0.23.2 are pinned so AutoProcessor registers `Glm5NextProcessor` for image and video inputs (cite75†#15751 ).
L198: ### Recipes
L199: 
L200:   * GLM-5.3-Flash SGLang recipes: Added `recipes/glm-5.3-flash/sglang` — shared model-cache PVC and pinned Hugging Face download, B200 aggregated (`agg-b200-agentic`, 1x TP4), GB200 aggregated (`agg-gb200-agentic`, 1x TP4), and GB200 disaggregated (`disagg-gb200-agentic`, 2 prefill TP4/EP4 + 1 decode TP4, Mooncake over NVLink) profiles, real EAGLE verification by default, and an AIPerf text-trace reference (cite78†recipe folder , cite79†README , cite76†#15752 , cite77†#16387 ).
L201: ## Getting Started
L202: Deployment is Kubernetes-based (Dynamo Platform and `DynamoGraphDeployment`). GB200 profiles also create a ComputeDomain and require the NVIDIA DRA driver. Full steps — namespace, `hf-token-secret`, model-cache PVC, pinned Hugging Face download, Kustomize render, DGD apply, and a `/v1/chat/completions` smoke test — are in the cite79†recipe README . The SGLang checkpoint is `RadixArk/GLM-5.3-Flash-NVFP4` and is not the checkpoint used by the sibling vLLM recipes.
L203: Recipes expect registry credentials for `nvcr.io`.
L204: ## Known Issues / Limitations
L205:   * Experimental snapshot build — not QA-gated; not for production.
L206:   * The disaggregated GB200 recipe accepts text and images only. Video input is available on the aggregated B200 and GB200 recipes.
L207:   * Disaggregated GB200 workers must share one NVLink clique and require the ComputeDomain CRD and NVIDIA DRA driver.
L208:   * Synthetic acceptance replaces real speculative verification and is for performance experiments only.
L209:   * End-to-end multimodal generation with this rebuilt image has not been validated. The image build checks that the GLM image and video processors load.
L210:   * Measured performance results are not included. The recipe ships an AIPerf text-trace job only.
