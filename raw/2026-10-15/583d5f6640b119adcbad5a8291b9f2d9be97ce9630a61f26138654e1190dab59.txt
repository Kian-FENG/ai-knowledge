L19: # Introducing Grok Voice 3.0
L20: 
L21: The world’s most intelligent voice model.
L22: 
L23: cite1†Try It Free†console.x.ai cite13†View Docs†docs.x.ai L24: Today, we’re announcing Grok Voice 3.0, the world’s most intelligent speech-to-speech model. It sets a new record for resolving customer support calls, has class-leading latency at under one second, and costs less than other frontier models. Grok Voice powers millions of conversations a day across Tesla and SpaceX, and is available today in the API at the same price as its predecessor.
L25: ## cite14†The Most Accurate Voice Model L26: 
L27: In Artificial Analysis’s independent testing, Grok Voice 3.0 ranks #1 on the Speech-to-Speech Index, with a score of 86.9. The Index combines speech reasoning, τ-Voice, and preference and task success from blind head-to-head calls. Grok Voice 3.0 also scores 97% on Full Duplex Bench, a measure of how naturally it handles pauses, turn-taking, and interruptions.

L54: ## cite17†Used by Millions of Customers at Tesla & SpaceX L55: 
L56: Grok Voice 3.0 handles millions of conversations a day in Tesla vehicles. Drivers can use Connectors for simple requests like checking their calendar or answering an email, and Grok Bot for bigger tasks like ordering a coffee or booking dinner—all completely hands-free (cite18†see it on X†x.com ). Grok Voice 3.0 also powers Grok Bot’s voice mode.
L57: Starlink call centers use Grok Voice to answer 20,000 customer calls every day across multiple languages. Roughly 70% are resolved with no human in the loop, and one in four sales calls ends in a purchase before the caller hangs up. A single agent built on Grok Voice 3.0 verifies customers, troubleshoots hardware, issues credits, and ships replacement equipment.
L58: ## cite19†Pricing and Availability L59: 
L60: Grok Voice 3.0 is available today in the Voice Agent API at cite20†$0.08 / min ($4.80 / hr)†docs.x.ai , the same price as Grok Voice 2.0. Agents using `grok-voice-latest` have already been switched over automatically, with no changes needed.
