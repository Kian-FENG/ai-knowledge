Eligible academic labs at U.S. universities can apply for the Rosalind Academic Plan: up to 30 Business Standard seats at $0 per user per month for up to 12 months. GPT-Rosalind requires trusted-access approval. API access is not included. Labs at universities with an existing OpenAI Enterprise agreement must apply through their university Enterprise process. Research data is not used to train models by default.
