Uber announced the company-wide rollout of ChatGPT Work, an agent within ChatGPT Enterprise, starting today. The rollout will make it available to more than 30,000 employees across Uber’s global workforce.
Expanding from an initial pilot of 1,000 employees, it is Uber’s first company-wide deployment of an agentic platform tailored for knowledge work.
It connects with Google Drive, Gmail, Google Calendar and Slack. GPT-6.1 Sol will be deployed as the default capability within ChatGPT Work.
Uber’s internal AI skills library, approved integrations and dedicated employee training support rollout. Software engineering teams will continue using Codex.
The companies plan to share further insights into adoption, use cases and measurable outcomes as rollout progresses.
