Personal artificial intelligence wearable company Nirva Labs today announced it has raised $25 million in early funding. The company is backed by Lightspeed Venture Partners, South Park Commons and others. Today’s round brings total funding to $33 million, following an $8 million seed round.
Its first product is AI-powered jewelry intended to journal a user’s day, identify patterns in moods and relationships and provide advice through a companion app. Wei Lyu is identified as co-founder and CEO.
The wearable primarily recognizes the user’s voice; with permission, calendar, location and Apple Health can supply context.
Orders open in November, with pricing expected at $249. First pieces are expected to ship by year end.
