L185: ## Questions about Healthleap
L186: 
L187: What is Healthleap?+
L188: 
L189: Healthleap provides inpatient risk-screening for hospitals. Its widely adopted malnutrition module is the only validated screening tool of its kind. It analyzes adult inpatient EHR data to identify patients who may be at nutritional risk and help registered dietitians prioritize patients for early intervention.
L190: 
L191: How does daily malnutrition screening work?+
L192: Healthleap analyzes adult inpatient charts each day and writes a nutrition-risk score into the EHR flowsheet each morning. Clinical nutrition teams can use the scores to prioritize patients for assessment within their existing workflow.
L193: 
L194:   * 【6†Read the clinical evidence】
L195: Does Healthleap diagnose malnutrition or replace clinicians?+
L196: No. Healthleap is clinical decision support, not a diagnostic tool, and it does not provide treatment recommendations. Its risk signals support registered dietitians' assessments. Clinicians remain responsible for clinical decisions and patient care.
L197: 
L198: What evidence supports Healthleap's malnutrition screening?+
L199: Co-authored research with leaders from Cedars-Sinai, Johns Hopkins, and Stanford, and published in Applied Clinical Informatics. To our knowledge, Healthleap offers the only commercially available, validated AI malnutrition screening tool. The Evidence page separates peer-reviewed clinical validation from hospital deployment and finance-reviewed results.
