TSMC September 2026 Revenue Report. Issued by TSMC. Issued on 2026/10/08.
HSINCHU, Taiwan, R.O.C. – October 8, 2026 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenue for September 2026: On a consolidated basis, revenue for September 2026 was approximately NT$511.86 billion, a decrease of 0.6 percent from August 2026 and an increase of 54.6 percent from September 2025. Revenue for January through September 2026 totaled NT$3,898.73 billion, an increase of 41.1 percent compared to the same period in 2025.
