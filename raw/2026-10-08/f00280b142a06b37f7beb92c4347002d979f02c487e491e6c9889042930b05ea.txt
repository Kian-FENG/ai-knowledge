Title: A NOTE ON OUR FUNDRAISE
Publication label: October 2026 (month only).

Nous Research raised $90 million to create AI that serves the people. We brought together some of the strongest investors in technology – NVIDIA, Microsoft’s M12, Samsung, Robot Ventures, Union Square Ventures, Y Combinator, and Menlo Ventures among others – to advance our mission, and to bring that mission to new domains.

We formed Nous in 2023 to combat the seemingly inexorable trend dragging AI toward centralization and walled gardens.

We released Hermes Agent with an MIT license this past February, because we believe tomorrow's moats will be built around execution, trust, and extensibility.

With our fundraising and new partners, we will build and deliver Hermes for Businesses.
